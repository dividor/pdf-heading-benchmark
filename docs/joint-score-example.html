Example: a document with four headings

Assume all headings are on the same PDF page.

Reference headings

H1  Summary
H1  Findings
    H2  School attendance
H1  Recommendations

Predicted headings

H1  Summary
H1  Findings
H1  School attendance
    H2  Attendance rose 12%
  • Correct: Summary and Findings have the right level.
  • Wrong level: School attendance should be H2.
  • Extra heading: Attendance rose 12% is body text.
  • Missing: Recommendations was not predicted.

C = 2 correct pairs, P = 4 predictions, G = 4 reference headings.

Joint F0.5 = (1.25 × 2) / (4 + 0.25 × 4) = 50%.

The wrong level earns no credit. The extra heading increases P, and the missing heading remains part of G. Four correct predictions with nothing extra score 100%.
